FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Brandloop runs in the browser on desktop or phone. You only need a free Claude account with the email we invited.

Can other clients see my brand?

No. Each user only sees the brands assigned to them, and each brand’s data is encrypted with its own key.

Who creates the content?

Brandloop’s AI drafts it from your brand profile and the market research, and the consulting team reviews it. Your team gives the final approval.

What happens in the first call?

We review your brand, your current channels and your goals, then show you what your Brandloop workspace would include.

How do I get my username?

Your Brandloop consultant creates it when your brand joins and shares it with you privately.
